Inside the Soft-Skills Guide

Explore the learning modules

A preview of topics covered in the soft-skills curriculum. Open any module to see what it includes.

01Introduction to soft skillsWhat they are and why they matter

How communication, attitude and interpersonal habits work alongside technical knowledge — and why they shape how others experience working with you.

02Proxemics and personal spaceIntimate, personal, social and public zones

An introduction to the four commonly described space zones and how distance affects comfort in conversations, meetings and interviews.

Comfortable and appropriate distance varies by culture, context and individual preference — these zones are a guide for awareness, not universal rules.

03Self-esteemA steady foundation for confidence

Recognising your strengths, managing self-talk and building confidence through preparation and small, repeated wins.

04IPS-15: interpersonal habitsFifteen habits for better relationships
  1. Be responsible
  2. Choose your words carefully
  3. Never be sarcastic
  4. Practise humility
  5. Discuss but never argue
  6. Stop playing the blame game
  7. Never complain or criticise
  8. Turn your promise into commitment
  9. Never gossip
  10. Never bear grudges
  11. Be grateful, never expect gratitude
  12. Be considerate
  13. Accept mistakes willingly and at once
  14. Think win-win
  15. Develop a sense of humour
05Face language and public imageExpression, presentation and first impressions

How facial expression, grooming and presentation contribute to the impression you create — and how to present yourself authentically and professionally.

06Personality and attitudeHow attitude shows up at work

Understanding the link between attitude, behaviour and professional relationships, and practising a constructive, solution-focused outlook.

07Smart-12: professionalism guidelinesTwelve habits for professional settings
  1. Be comfortable speaking with new people
  2. Make a good first impression
  3. Maintain attentive eye contact
  4. Use confident posture
  5. Be approachable
  6. Become a thoughtful conversationalist
  7. Listen actively
  8. Stay in touch professionally
  9. Participate in social and professional settings
  10. Face fears constructively
  11. Be proactive
  12. Stay open-minded and respectful
08Kinesics and body languageHandshake, posture, gestures and more
  • Handshake
  • Arm crossing
  • Eye contact
  • Posture
  • Facial expression
  • Hand gestures

Body-language cues depend on context and culture. They can help you communicate more clearly, but they are not a reliable way to read someone’s thoughts or intentions.

09Managing a group during a presentationHolding attention and handling questions

Practical techniques for engaging an audience, keeping a session on track, managing questions and staying composed in front of a group.

10Understanding peoplePerspective, empathy and listening

Seeing situations from other people’s point of view, asking better questions and responding with empathy.

11Barriers to effective people skillsWhat gets in the way — and how to work around it

Common obstacles such as assumptions, poor listening, defensiveness and unclear expression, with practical ways to reduce them.
